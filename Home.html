History of Aklan

Aklan has a long history shaped by Indigenous communities, migration, trade, and Spanish colonial rule. It was once part of Capiz. Republic Act No. 1414 created the Province of Aklan on April 25, 1956.

See more

The Ati-Atihan Festival is one of Aklan’s best-known cultural celebrations. It honors the Santo Niño and is associated with local traditions and stories about early communities on Panay.

The Code of Kalantiaw was once described as an ancient law code connected to Batan, Aklan. The National Historical Commission of the Philippines found it had no valid historical basis, so it should not be treated as verified history.

General Ananias Diokno Shrine

A historical site in Barangay Dalipdip commemorating General Ananias Diokno. American forces captured him in Altavas in 1901.

Agtutoe-an Falls

A waterfall in Barangay Talon. Its cool water and natural setting make it a local outing spot.

Macawiwili Falls

A waterfall in Barangay Cabugao. It is one of Altavas’s natural attractions.

Kipot Cave

A cave in Barangay Talon; kipot means “narrow” in the local description. Check locally about access before visiting.

Balete

Agtawagon Hill

It served as a camp and last line of defense for Filipino soldiers and guerrillas during World War II. It’s a historical spot in Balete.

Basura Garden

This private garden displays art made from antiques and repurposed materials. Check with the owner or local tourism office about visiting.

Kabuhi-an Center

The center supports skills training and showcases Aklanon products and crafts. It promotes local livelihoods and handmade goods.

Banga

Manduyog Hill

The hill has life-size Stations of the Cross winding up its slope. It overlooks the Aklan State University campus.

Batan

Ob-Ob Hill

This hill rises above the Sibuyan Sea and resembles a lighthouse. A sandy beach lies below it.

Batan beaches and Coral Garden

Batan’s coast includes beaches in Songcolan, Mandong, Napti, and Mambuquiao.

Camanci Falls

Camanci Falls is a natural water attraction in Batan. Its surroundings offer a quiet place to enjoy the scenery.

Buruanga

Ingus-Ingus Hill

It served as a coastal lookout during the Spanish era. A cave lies beneath the hill.

Ashram Balay Pagduaw

This is a quiet place for meditation, reflection, and retreats. Visitors can spend time in its natural setting.

Saint Anthony of Padua Parish Church

This century-old church is an important place of worship in Buruanga. It is dedicated to Saint Anthony and Nuestra Señora de Salvacion.

Ariel’s Point

This coastal attraction is known for cliff diving and swimming spots. Visitors also come for snorkeling and marine life.

Sabang Cave

This elevated cave offers views of nearby beaches and the sea. Check local access guidance before visiting.

Sapsapon Cave

The cave contains stalagmites and stalactites formed over time. Ask locally about access and guided visits.

Tigis Falls

Tigis Falls is a natural waterfall and swimming destination. Follow local safety guidance around the water.

Talisay Beach

Talisay is a coastal beach known for clear water. Check sea and weather conditions before swimming.

Hinugtan White Beach

Hinugtan is a beach destination for swimming and snorkeling. Its coastal setting is suited to a nature visit.

Tuburan Baybay

Tuburan Baybay is a beach area for relaxing by the coast. Visitors can enjoy swimming and snorkeling.

Pagatpat Mangrove

This mangrove area has a boardwalk through coastal greenery. It offers a calm place to explore the local environment.

Hacienda Maria

Hacienda Maria is a nature destination with caves and waterfalls. Local visits may include seeing how lambanog is made.

Ibajay

Katunggan it Ibajay

This mangrove area is known for its variety of mangrove species. It is also used for mangrove and aquaculture research.

Campo Verde

Campo Verde is a forested highland area along the Ibajay-Tangalan border. Its wooded trails attract hikers.

Ibajay Beach

This shoreline stretches across several coastal barangays. It is a local place for picnics and swimming.

Ibajay River

The river runs from upland barangays toward the coast. Its rapids and boulders draw water-sports and rafting enthusiasts.

Snibaa Falls

Snibaa Falls is reached by a short hike through the mountains. Its cool water makes a refreshing stop after exploring.

Alejandro Melchor’s Birthplace

Ibajay is the birthplace of engineer Alejandro Melchor. He is remembered for his work on military pontoon bridges during World War II.

Kalibo

Aklan Freedom Shrine

The shrine honors the XIX Martyrs of Aklan. It is a memorial site in Kalibo.

Tigayon Hill and Caves

Tigayon Hill is a hiking spot with caves and local history. The trail offers views of the surrounding area.

Museo it Akean

This museum displays artifacts and items connected to Aklanon culture. It is a place to learn about Aklan’s heritage.

Piña Village

Visitors can see how pineapple fibers are turned into piña cloth. The village is in Barangay New Buswang.

Kalibo Cathedral

The cathedral is one of the province’s historic churches. It remains an important place of worship in Kalibo.

Bakhawan Eco-Park

This mangrove eco-park has an elevated walkway through the forest. Visitors can also enjoy nature walks and kayaking.

Lezo

Lezo Plaza

The plaza is a central gathering place for community events. Sports courts and municipal buildings are nearby.

Lezo Church

This historic church stands near the town plaza. It remains part of the town’s religious and community life.

Bayangan Village

Local potters make clay pots and jars using traditional methods. Ask local makers about seeing the craft.

Libacao

Taroytoy

Taroytoy is a forested upland area with cooler weather and hilly terrain. Reaching it involves a long hike from Manika.

The Wild River

The upstream Aklan River is known for clear water and a rugged setting. Local activities may include rafting and kayaking.

Nasoraan Waterfalls

Libacao has waterfalls in its upland areas. Ask local guides about routes and current access before setting out.

Inland Adventure Trek

This trek follows the river upstream from Manika toward Oyang. Plan the hike with local guidance.

Mountaineering Trails

Local routes lead toward Taroytoy, Mount Nausan, and Mount Nangtud. These hikes require preparation and a local guide.

Madalag

Liktinon White Rocks

These pale boulders and rock pools lie along the Timbaban River. The area is surrounded by a forested valley.

Panipiason Hanging Bridge

The bridge crosses a scenic upland area in Panipiason. It is a popular stop for visitors exploring the countryside.

Agtughangin Falls

Agtughangin Falls is a cold-water waterfall in Panipiason. Forest scenery surrounds the falls.

Makato

Aklan Sports Complex

The complex has a track and field oval, swimming pool, and other sports facilities. It is located in Makato.

Salimuno Falls

Salimuno Falls is in Barangay Libang, several kilometers from the town center. Its clear water makes it a local nature stop.

Baybay Beach

Baybay Beach is a coastal destination in Makato. Check local access and travel guidance before visiting.

Malay

Malay Ecological Park

The park in Barangay Argao has fruit trees and a butterfly farm. It also offers views toward Boracay Island.

Pangihan Cave

Pangihan Cave has several connected chambers. Check with local guides about access before visiting.

Bat Caves

These caves are home to fruit-eating bats and are reached by hiking or biking. Local volunteers can guide visitors.

Preserved Dead Forest

This inland water area contains clusters of standing dead tree trunks. It offers an unusual natural landscape to view.

Malinao

Candido Iban Monument

The monument honors Candido Iban, a Katipunero and one of the XIX Martyrs of Aklan. It is in his home municipality of Malinao.

Fatima Hill

Fatima Hill has Stations of the Cross along its slope and a chapel at the top. It can be reached by local transport.

Taeangban Hill

Taeangban Hill is located in Barangay Dangcalan. Just like Fatima Hills it also has Stations of the Cross along its slope. The top can be reached by motorcycle, walking, or other local transport.

Mampahon Falls

Manomong Falls is a waterfall in Barangay Tigpalas. Reaching it involves a hike upstream.

Nabas

Bungan-bungan Cold Springs

These cold springs are known for clear water flowing around large rock formations. They are popular for swimming in warm weather.

Basang Cave

Basang Cave is a long cave near Barangay Poblacion. Ask locally about access and whether a guide is needed.

Tagororoc Ecotourism Destination

Tagororoc is a nature destination with rivers, water basins, and waterfalls. It is suited to visitors looking for an outdoor trip.

New Washington

Tambak Sea Wall

The seawall is a spot for watching the sunrise along the coast. Nearby restaurants serve local seafood.

Pink Sisters Convent

The convent is in Barangay Polo. It is a religious destination visited by local devotees.

Jaime L. Sin Monument

This monument honors Cardinal Jaime L. Sin, who was born in New Washington. The bronze statue is in his hometown.

Isla Kapispisan

This mangrove area is a habitat for birds and aquatic life. It is associated with local conservation efforts.

Lagatik River Cruise

The cruise follows the scenic Lagatik River and nearby coastal areas. Tours may include mangroves and local fish or oyster farms.

Numancia

Century-Old Acacia Trees

A cluster of old acacia trees stands at Albasan Elementary School. The trees were planted in 1892.

Camanci Beach

Camanci is one of Numancia’s coastal barangays with beach areas. Check locally for access and swimming conditions.

Navitas Beach

Navitas is another coastal barangay in Numancia with beach areas. Follow local guidance when visiting.

Tangalan

Jawili Falls

Jawili Falls is a series of natural pools and cascades in Tangalan. Follow local safety guidance around the water.

Marine Sanctuary and Coral Garden

This protected coastal area supports coral and marine life. Ask locally about snorkeling access and conservation rules.

Afga Point

Afga Point is a coastal attraction in Tangalan. It offers a scenic place to enjoy the shoreline.

Campo Verde

Campo Verde is a forested highland area shared by Tangalan and Ibajay. Its wooded setting attracts hikers.

Bughawi Beach

Bughawi is one of Tangalan’s coastal beaches. Check local conditions before swimming.

Travel with Care

  • Check current travel guidance with official tourism offices before setting out.
  • Follow local instructions at beaches, waterfalls, marine areas, and heritage sites.
  • Support local guides, makers, and businesses, and ask before photographing people or cultural activities.
  • Take litter with you and help protect the coast, rivers, and upland environments.
